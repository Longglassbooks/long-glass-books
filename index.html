Return Incomplete

Thomas Rook used to fight wars through bodies waiting millions of kilometres away. Now he recovers Frames for a living in London. Then a routine job uncovers an erased CNDR operation, an impossible military core and a piece of his own past that should not exist.

The Concordance universe

Distance used to mean absence.

The Continuity Cycle explores a future in which human consciousness can inhabit distant bodies through Concordance, and the political, military and personal consequences of turning identity into infrastructure.

A body can be borrowed. A self can be distributed. The difficult question is what remains when connection stops being a choice.
