Long Glass Books

About

A small independent publishing imprint with an interest in speculative fiction, human-scale futures and stories where technology changes the terms without replacing the people.

Long Glass Books was established as an independent imprint for fiction that values character, consequence and a world large enough to feel lived in.

Its first publications are Return Incomplete and What Remains by A. J. Malan, the opening volumes of The Continuity Cycle, set within the wider Concordance universe.

The imprint is deliberately small. Long Glass Books is not currently operating an open submissions programme and cannot accept unsolicited manuscripts.

Publishing identity

Long Glass Books is the publishing imprint shown on its editions and associated metadata. The public website is longglassbooks.uk.
