Book One of The Continuity Cycle

Return Incomplete

Thomas Rook used to fight wars through bodies waiting millions of kilometres away. Now he recovers Frames for a living in London. When a routine recovery leads him to an erased CNDR operation and a military Concordance core that recognises him, Rook is pulled back towards a history he cannot remember and institutions that would prefer it stayed buried.

His path converges with salvager Ada Mercer and pilot Julian Cross, who have recovered another fragment of the same past. Between them lies a body the Crown believes it owns, an old soldier who may not have returned whole, and a question nobody has managed to kill.

Book Two of The Continuity Cycle

What Remains

Thomas Rook’s biological body is on the Moon. The body Ada Mercer and Julian Cross know is somewhere else entirely: a recovered military primary the Crown still claims, carrying a soldier it thought it had finished with years ago.

After Cairn, the three keep moving while quieter forms of pressure close around them: insurance, berths, contracts and legal authority. Their search leads towards Lantern House, a buried CNDR station and the remains of an operation that has been waiting for Rook for ten years.
